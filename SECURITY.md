# Security Policy — MS-C Mantle

## Scope

MS-C is a **Preliminary Design + digital prototype** for site-deployable VIS/IR
adversarial camouflage research. It does **not** include laser emitters, live
weapon interfaces, or classified threat models.

## Reporting

Report security issues to **kylebean@fratres-x.com**. Do not open public issues
for export-sensitive or operationally sensitive material.

## Expectations

- Do not treat digital MoE scores as fielded effectiveness.
- Do not use this repository as authority to manufacture, export, or field
  camouflage systems for operational use without separate legal review.
