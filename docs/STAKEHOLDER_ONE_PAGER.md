# Stakeholder One-Pager — MS-C Mantle

**Send after tour / call.** Conceptual study. Not a buy decision package.

---

## What it is

Site-deployable **adversarial camouflage / EO+IR deception** pattern kits for
critical infrastructure — coatings, nets, tile sheets. Passive DENY layer under
a Kill-Web style stack.

## What it is not

- Not a laser dazzler (see **MPL-D**)
- Not a multispectral smoke grenade (see **MS-V Veil**)
- Not a claim of invisible sites or validated UAS defeat rates

## Why now

Public CUAS discussion already pairs **dazzlers + adversarial camo** as early
for mainstream. Homeland densification needs cheap, producible soft layers
before kinetic. Mantle covers the **persistent site** gap.
