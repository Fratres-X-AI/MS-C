# 00 — Executive Brief — MS-C Mantle

**Document ID:** MS-C-EB-001 · **Maturity:** Preliminary Design + digital prototype  
**Organization:** Fratres X AI — Defense Projects

---

## Problem

Group 1–2 UAS keep EO/IR “eyes” on soft and critical sites. Transient smoke fails
against thermal. EW fails against fiber. Kinetic does not scale cheaply for
densified homeland CUAS. Sites also lack a **persistent, passive** way to deny
detectors when no effector is pulsing and no grenade is thrown.

## Concept

**MS-C Mantle** generates manufacturable **VIS + LWIR-surrogate** adversarial
pattern kits — nets, coatings, and tile sheets — optimized to degrade modern
aerial detectors on substations, pads, and pipeline corridors.

## Layered stack

| Layer | Product | Persistence |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| Passive deny | **MS-C Mantle** | Weeks+ (inspect/refresh) |
| Transient obscure | MS-V Veil | ~120 s |
| Directed dazzle | MPL-D | Pulse |
| Kinetic | MFKS / RADR | Engagement |
